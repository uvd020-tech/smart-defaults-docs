## Contact

Email [info@vudia.site](mailto:info@vudia.site). We aim to reply within two
business days.

To help us reproduce the problem, please include:

1. What you were doing, and what happened instead of what you expected.
2. Your Jira site URL (for example `yourcompany.atlassian.net`).
3. The project and the issue type, and the fields that were not filled.
4. Whether you are a Jira administrator, a project administrator or a regular
   user, and whether the project is team-managed or company-managed.
5. The exact error message shown by the app, or a screenshot.
6. If the problem is in the Create dialog: open the browser console (**F12**)
   and copy the line that starts with `[smart-defaults]`. It lists the result for
   each field.

## Feature requests

Send them to the same address. Tell us what you are trying to achieve rather
than the solution you have in mind — it usually leads to a better fit.
